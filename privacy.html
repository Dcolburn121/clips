Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

YouTube API Services

Daily Clipz uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to, and read view counts for, the YouTube channel you connect. By connecting a YouTube channel you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

From YouTube, Daily Clipz only accesses: permission to upload videos to your channel, and the IDs, links and view counts of the videos it uploaded. It does not read your other videos, comments, subscribers, messages or watch history, and it does not share any YouTube data with third parties.

Revoking access: you can remove Daily Clipz's access to your Google/YouTube account at any time at Google security settings → Third-party apps & services.

What we collect

When you connect a social media account, we receive only what that platform shares with the permissions you approve:

  • Basic profile information: account ID, username, display name and avatar (for example TikTok's user.info.basic).
  • Access tokens: these let the app upload videos you approve.
  • Post information: IDs, links and view counts of videos the app published for you (for example TikTok's video.list).

We do not collect passwords, payment details, contacts, messages or location.

How we use it

Only to publish the videos you approve, show your posting settings, and show how your posts are performing. We do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Information goes only to the platform you are posting to, as part of uploading your video.

Storage and security

Data is stored privately on the operator's own computer or server. Tokens are kept in access-restricted files and are never published.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is connected. YouTube view counts are refreshed every few hours, and view counts that have not been refreshed for 30 days are deleted automatically. When you revoke access, the stored access token stops working. You can also email us to have all stored YouTube data deleted, and we will do so within 7 days. You can disconnect the app at any time in the platform's settings, for example TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. You can also email us to delete everything stored about you, and we will do so within 30 days.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We will update the date above if this policy changes.
